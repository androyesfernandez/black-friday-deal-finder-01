How these deals were checked

Every deal on this page was found and checked by the finder’s maker on 27 November 2026 (Black Friday): the sale price was read on the store’s own product page, or in a deals roundup that names the store and the price. Each card names where its price was read and when. A discount is only ever the sale price against the usual price the store itself lists — no inflated “was” prices are used. Prices can change or sell out; the store page is the final word.

Verdict key: NEW LOW the lowest price the sources show for this product · STRONG PRICE a genuinely good price, at or near the best seen · ROUTINE SALE a real discount, but one this product sees often.
